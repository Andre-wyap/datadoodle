# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Established Malaysian SME owners aged 30–55, typically operating RM500k–RM10m businesses in healthcare, legal and professional services, education, property, and specialist trades. They are often reading on a phone, on mobile data, between other tasks. Their job is to quickly decide whether DataDoodles understands their visibility problem and is credible enough to assess it.

## Product Purpose

DataDoodles rebuilds Malaysian SME websites so customers can find, trust, and contact the business through Google and AI-assisted search. The landing page’s single conversion goal is a four-field submission for a free Findability Score. Success means the site itself demonstrates the clarity, structure, speed, accessibility, and conversion discipline being sold.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, candid, and lightly playful. Serious about commercial outcomes and transparent about prices and limitations, while using restrained hand-drawn annotations to make technical ideas understandable.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-agency aesthetics: dark purple gradient heroes, glassmorphism, floating 3D objects, neon AI motifs, stock photos of people pointing at laptops, nested cards, icon tiles, logo marquees, cream editorial layouts, childish crayon styling, and “most popular” pricing badges. Hand-drawn marks are an annotation layer, never the foundation.

## Design Principles

1. Practice what the service sells: fast, accessible, structured, and easy to understand.
2. Make the commercial outcome obvious within ten seconds.
3. Show evidence instead of relying on unverified claims.
4. Remove friction from every path to contact.
5. Use playfulness as a precise annotation, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and interaction requirements, use semantic headings and form labels, provide visible keyboard focus, link actionable errors to their fields, support reduced motion, use touch-friendly controls, and maintain legibility on small screens and mobile data connections.
