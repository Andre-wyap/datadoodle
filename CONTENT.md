## 02 — Our Services

### We get your website shown in 3 places.

**CTA:** Get your quote →

### 01. Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

The Google layer. Site structure, page speed, internal linking and topic coverage, so the pages you already have rank for what your customers actually type. It is the foundation the other two stand on: a model cannot cite a page a crawler never understood.

### 02. Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews don't rank ten blue links. They name one business, maybe three. We structure your entity, schema and proof so a model can state what you do, who you do it for and where you operate, then name you when someone asks for a recommendation.

### 03. Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)

Featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice assistants and Google Maps all pull a single direct answer. We write and mark up your pages so the answer they pull is yours: real questions, plain answers, details a machine can verify.

## 05 — The Process

### Five stages. One clear record at the end.

1. **Audit:** We record where you appear today across each major AI platform, and audit up to three named competitors appearing instead of you.
2. **Identify:** Our in-house AI tools identify the gaps: missing keywords, unclear information, questions your site never answers.
3. **Improve:** We rebuild your website, or build on top of what you already have, so a model can understand and cite your business.
4. **Deploy:** Your new site goes live within 30 days, structured, tested, and ready to be found.
5. **Report:** You get the before and after, per platform, with the exact wording each model used to describe you, plus the fixes still worth making next.

### Typical timeline: 30 days

Approximately. Audit and Identify run in the first week; Improve and Deploy take the rest. You get the report whether or not the answers moved.

## 08 — Questions

### Straight answers.

#### What is AI search optimisation?

It is the work of making your business understandable and citable by AI systems, so that when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google’s AI Overview for a recommendation, your business is one of the names it has to give.

#### Is this the same as SEO?

Related, but not the same job. SEO optimises for a position in a list of links. This optimises for being named inside an answer, where there is no page two and usually no more than three names.

#### How long does it take?

The typical engagement runs about 30 days from kickoff to the final report. Audit and Identify take the first week; Improve and Deploy take the rest.

#### Can you guarantee placement?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. AI answers vary by phrasing, location and model version, and they change without notice. What we guarantee is the work, the testing, and an honest before-and-after record of what we found.

#### How will I know whether it is working?

We re-run the same questions on the same platforms after the work lands, and show you the answers side by side with dates. You see the wording the model used, not a number we made up.

#### Do I still need a website?

Yes. Your website is where the models read from. If it does not clearly state what you do, who you serve and where, there is nothing for an answer to be built from.

#### Do I still need Meta ads?

If they work for you, keep them. Ads create awareness; AI visibility supports the research and trust step that happens after. They do different jobs.

#### Which AI platforms do you work with?

ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Gemini and Perplexity. Each is checked and reported separately, because the same question can produce four different answers.
